## **Brand Guidelines & Design System**

### **Color Palette (UPDATED)**
- **Primary**: Oxford Blue (#002147) - Authority, trust, academic excellence
- **Accent**: Goldenrod (#D4A017) - Achievement, prestige, opportunity  
- **Supporting**: Gray scale (#F8F9FA to #212529) - Professional hierarchy
- **Semantic**: Success (#28A745), Warning (#FFC107), Error (#DC3545), Info (#17A2B8)

### **Typography System**
- **Headlines & Navigation**: Work Sans (modern, professional, accessible)
- **Body Text**: Work Sans (consistency, readability, performance)
- **Articles & Quotes**: Source Serif Pro (authority, academic tradition)
- **Monospace**: Roboto Mono (data, metrics, technical content)

### **Visual Hierarchy**
- **Professional Photography**: Real campus, students, faculty (no stock photos)
- **Data Visualization**: Charts, metrics, placement statistics
- **Brand Consistency**: STIE Dwimulya logo prominence
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance
- **Mobile-First**: Responsive design prioritizing mobile experience

### **Content Strategy**
- **Transparency**: Public dashboard untuk semua metrics
- **Credibility**: BAN-PT accreditation numbers, verified statistics
- **Local Pride**: Banten regional identity dengan national aspiration
- **Student-Centric**: Success stories, real outcomes, career progression
- **Professional Tone**: Academic authority tanpa emoji atau casual language

- **Modular Data**: Page-specific data files untuk maintainability
