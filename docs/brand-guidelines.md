## 1. Strategi Merek Kami

### 1.1 Janji Utama Kami
Bagi lulusan SMA di Banten yang memimpikan karier dari level regional hingga global namun menghadapi kendala finansial, STIE Dwimulya adalah sekolah tinggi ekonomi & bisnis terdepan yang menjamin beasiswa penuh dan memberikan pendampingan kerja terdokumentasi selama 6 bulan (dengan gaji minimal UMK Banten).

**Disclaimer Legal**: *Semua jaminan tunduk pada syarat dan ketentuan lengkap yang tersedia di stiedwimulya.ac.id/terms*

### 1.2 Perbandingan Kompetitif

| Kategori | Universitas Swasta Terkemuka Banten | Politeknik Negeri Serang | **STIE Dwimulya** |
|----------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Batas Beasiswa | 25% (parsial) | 0% | **60% (penuh)** |
| Rata-rata Waktu Kerja | 9-12 bulan | 8-10 bulan | **<6 bulan** |
| Tracer Study Publik | Tidak tersedia | Terbatas | **Dashboard real-time** |
| Jaminan Tertulis | Tidak ada | Tidak ada | **Ya, dengan kompensasi** |

*(Metodologi penelitian: Survei 2024 terhadap 150 alumni dari 5 institusi, margin error ±3.2%)*

### 1.3 Definisi Legal "Jaminan Karier 6 Bulan"

Kami berjanji menyediakan setiap lulusan dengan:
- **Minimum 3 wawancara** yang dimediasi pusat karier kami
- **Penawaran kerja tertulis** dengan gaji ≥ UMK Banten saat itu
- **Jika tidak terpenuhi**: 1 tahun kursus peningkatan keahlian gratis + fasilitasi 15 wawancara tambahan

**Transparansi Publik**: Kemajuan dilacak di [stiedwimulya.ac.id/guarantee-dashboard](http://stiedwimulya.ac.id/guarantee-dashboard)

### 1.4 Metrik Keberhasilan 2026

| Metrik | Target | Sumber Data | Review |
|--------|--------|-------------|---------|
| Penerima beasiswa penuh/angkatan | 60% | Dept. Keuangan | Semesteran |
| Tingkat penempatan kerja 6 bulan | ≥85% | Tracer Study | Tahunan |
| Rata-rata gaji awal | ≥1.2× UMK Banten | Survei Employer | Tahunan |

## 2. Suara & Pesan Kami

### 2.1 Hierarki Pesan
**Pesan Puncak**: *"Beasiswa 100% – Karier 6 Bulan – Dari Serang, Untuk Indonesia"*

**Tiga Pilar Bukti** *(gunakan berurutan)*:
1. **60% kursi beasiswa penuh** untuk prestasi akademik + need-based
2. **85% lulusan dipekerjakan <6 bulan** (Tracer Study 2024)
3. **Kurikulum co-created** dengan 15+ perusahaan MoU

### 2.2 Panduan Nada Bicara

| Konteks | Katakan Ini ✅ | Hindari Ini ❌ |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| Instagram Hook | "Kuliah ekonomi GRATIS di Serang!" | "Murah & terjangkau" |
| Billboard CTA | "Daftar Beasiswa 100%" | "Diskon besar-besaran" |
| Crisis Response | "Kami terbuka untuk audit independen..." | "Itu adalah fitnah" |

| Competitor Reference | "Kami fokus pada transparansi jaminan" | *Hindari menyebut nama kompetitor* |

### 2.3 Strategi Komunikasi Krisis

**Tim Krisis**: Rektor + Kepala Komunikasi + Brand Guardian

**Response Timeline**:
- **0-30 menit**: Holding statement melalui official channels
- **1-2 jam**: Detailed response dengan data pendukung
- **24 jam**: Follow-up action plan
- **72 jam**: Stakeholder communication & lesson learned

**Kriteria Eskalasi Krisis**:
- Sentimen negatif >15% dalam 24 jam (diukur via Brandwatch)
- Mention negatif >100 dalam 4 jam
- Media massa pickup dengan fact-checking requirement
- Ancaman legal atau regulatory

## 3. Identitas Visual Kami

### 3.1 Logo Suite (5 Variasi)

**A. Primary Horizontal** - Default untuk semua aplikasi  
**B. Stacked Vertical** - Ruang persegi/vertikal  
**C. Icon Only** - Favicon, profile pics (safe hingga 16×16px)  
**D. Reversed** - Putih penuh untuk background gelap  
**E. Monochrome** - Satu warna untuk laser engraving/embossing  

**Safe Zone**: 0.5× tinggi ikon di semua sisi  
**Minimum Size**: 24px digital, 12mm print

### 3.2 Logo Violations
❌ **Jangan pernah**: stretch, rotate, drop-shadow, gradient, outline  
❌ **Background kontras rendah**: <4.5:1 ratio  
❌ **Cluttered placement**: pada foto busy tanpa treatment

### 3.3 Color Palette & Accessibility

**Primary Colors**:
- **Oxford Blue** #002147 (Pantone 282 C) - 60% usage
- **Golden Rod** #D4A017 (Pantone 7407 C) - 20% usage  
- **Broken White** #F8F8F8 - 20% usage

**WCAG 2.1 AA Compliant Combinations**:
- ✅ Oxford Blue text on Broken White (12.6:1)
- ✅ Golden Rod text on Oxford Blue (4.8:1)
- ❌ Golden Rod text on Broken White (2.9:1) - avoid

**Seasonal Campaign Colors** *(max 2 minggu)*:
- Ramadan: Sage Green #9DB5A3
- Graduation: Coral #FF6F61
- New Student: Sky Blue #87CEEB

### 3.4 Typography System

**Primary Serif**: Source Serif Pro (headlines, quotes, emphasis)  
**Primary Sans**: Work Sans (body, UI, captions)  
**Data/Numbers**: Roboto Mono (statistics, tables)

**Scale** *(desktop/mobile)*:
- H1: 40px/34px
- H2: 32px/28px  
- H3: 28px/24px
- Body: 16px/14px
- Caption: 12px/11px

**Line Height**: 1.5× font size  
**Accessibility**: Min 16px body text, max 75 characters per line

### 3.5 Photography Standards

**Technical Requirements**:
- **Format**: RAW capture, deliver RGB JPEGs ≥24MP
- **Lighting**: Natural light preferred, 5600K temperature
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, eye-level perspective
- **Resolution**: Min 3000px wide untuk web hero images

**Diversity & Representation Guidelines**:
- 40% perempuan, 30% berhijab, 20% visible disabilities
- Age range: 18-50 tahun
- Authentic expressions, no overly-staged poses
- Campus environment dengan brand colors sebagai accent

**Forbidden Styles**:
- Smartphone selfie quality
- Harsh flash shadows
- Visible stock photo watermarks
- Overly filtered/processed looks

### 3.6 Co-Branding dengan Partners

**Layout Standard**: STIE logo (kiri) + 1px Golden Rod divider + Partner logo (kanan)  
**Size Ratio**: 1:1 atau STIE slightly larger (1.2:1)  
**Minimum Height**: 48px untuk co-brand lockup  
**Legal**: Requires signed MoU dengan brand usage clause

## 5. Content & Social Media Strategy

### 5.1 Platform-Specific Guidelines

| Platform | Profile Image | Cover/Header | Hashtag Limit | Best Posting Time (WIB) | Primary Audience |
|----------|---------------|---------------|---------------|-------------------------|------------------|
| Instagram | Icon 500×500px | Story 1080×1920 | 5-7 | 18:00-20:00 | Prospective students |
| TikTok | Icon 200×200px | N/A | 3-4 | 12:00 & 19:00 | Gen Z engagement |
| LinkedIn | Icon 400×400px | 1584×396 | 3-5 | 08:00-09:00 | Parents, employers |
| Facebook | Icon 500×500px | 820×312 | 3-5 | 19:00-21:00 | Parents, community |

### 5.2 Weekly Content Calendar

**Senin - #SeninSiapKerja**  
*Format*: Tips carousel 5-10 slides  
*Topics*: Interview prep, CV writing, workplace skills  
*CTA*: "Siap kerja dalam 6 bulan? DM kami!"

**Selasa - Myth vs Fact Tuesday**  
*Format*: Split-screen design atau Before/After  
*Topics*: Scholarship misconceptions, career myths  
*CTA*: "Cari tahu fakta lengkapnya di link bio"

**Rabu - Alumni Spotlight**  
*Format*: 30-second Reels dengan subtitle  
*Content*: Name, graduation year, current role, salary range  
*CTA*: "Kamu bisa jadi yang selanjutnya!"

**Kamis - Curriculum Peek**  
*Format*: Behind-the-scenes video atau infographic  
*Topics*: Industry partnerships, practical learning  
*CTA*: "Mau belajar dari praktisi? Daftar sekarang!"

**Jumat - Live Q&A**  
*Format*: Instagram/TikTok Live 30-45 menit  
*Host*: Student ambassadors + academic staff  
*Topics*: Admissions, scholarships, campus life

### 5.3 Student Voice Integration

**Student Ambassador Program**:
- 10 students dari berbagai program studi
- Monthly content quota: 2 posts, 1 story takeover
- Brand training mandatory setiap semester
- Performance bonus: best engagement rate

**Testimonial Usage Guidelines**:
- Obtain written consent dengan specific usage terms
- Include full name, program, graduation year
- Add disclaimer: *"Individual results may vary"*
- Review/update testimonials annually

### 5.4 Crisis Social Media Protocols

**Level 1 - Minor Issues** (isolated complaints):
- Respond within 2 jam dengan empathetic tone
- Move conversation to DM when possible
- Document di shared Crisis Log

**Level 2 - Trending Negative** (viral criticism):
- Activate 30-minute response protocol
- Koordinasi dengan Brand Guardian before posting
- Prepared statements untuk common scenarios

**Level 3 - Major Crisis** (institutional reputation threat):
- All social media paused except official statements
- Redirect ke official crisis communication channels
- Monitor 24/7 dengan escalation ke leadership

## 8. Measurement & ROI

### 8.1 Brand Tracking Methodology

**Quantitative Research** *(Kantar, quarterly)*:
- Sample: n=400, age 17-19, Banten residents
- Key metrics: Aided/unaided recall, brand attributes, consideration
- Benchmark: Q4 2024 baseline establishment
- Target: 35% unaided recall by Q4 2026

**Qualitative Research** *(Focus groups, bi-annual)*:
- 6 groups of 8 participants each
- Mix of current students, parents, employers
- Topics: Brand perception, messaging effectiveness, competitive positioning

### 8.2 Digital Performance Tracking

**Social Media KPIs**:
- Follower growth rate: >15% annually
- Engagement rate: >6% average
- Brand mention sentiment: >70% positive
- DM conversion to inquiry: >25%

**Website Analytics**:
- Bounce rate: <40% 
- Time on site: >2:30 average
- Conversion rate (inquiry form): >12%
- Organic search traffic growth: >20% annually

### 8.3 ROI Calculation Framework

**Formula**: `ROI = [(Additional Enrollment × Lifetime Student Value) - Campaign Costs] / Campaign Costs`

**Variables**:
- **Lifetime Student Value**: Rp 45,000,000 (4 years tuition)
- **Attribution Window**: 12 bulan untuk brand campaign impact
- **Target ROI**: ≥200% (adjusted from 300% for realistic expectations)

**Cost Centers**:
- Digital advertising spend
- Content creation costs
- Brand asset development
- Staff time allocation (calculated at hourly rates)
